### Manage Your Profile

## Booking a Shipment

### How to Book

1. Open the client home screen.
2. Enter pickup and drop locations.
3. Choose inter-city or intra-city transport.
4. Select the truck type that fits your shipment.
5. Add package details, weight, and any required timing details.
6. Review the estimated price.
7. Confirm the booking and complete the required payment step.

### Pickup and Drop Details

Please make sure the pickup and drop addresses are accurate. Incorrect addresses, missing contact details, access restrictions, or delays at loading/unloading points may affect the trip timeline and final charges.

### Truck Selection

Truck availability depends on location, route, load type, and active partners. If a selected truck is unavailable, the app may search for nearby suitable trucks or route the booking through broker/driver assignment.

## Pricing and Payments

### Fare Estimate

The estimate may include distance, truck category, transport type, route conditions, availability, express delivery options, halting, and other applicable charges.

Final payable amounts may change if:

- Pickup or drop location changes
- Shipment weight or material details change
- Extra waiting or halting time is added
- Express delivery or special handling is selected
- Taxes, payment charges, or platform charges apply

### Payment Options

Depending on the booking and payment stage, you may see options such as:

- Pay Now: Pay the full amount online.
- Advance: Pay a partial amount now and the remaining balance later.
- To Pay: Pay the full amount at delivery.
- To Be Billed: Payment is settled separately as agreed with the platform or business account.

Available payment options may vary by booking, route, account type, and platform rules.

### Payment Failure

If money was deducted but the booking still shows unpaid:

1. Wait a few minutes and refresh the booking.
2. Check your payment app or bank statement.
3. Do not make repeated payments unless the app clearly asks you to retry.
4. Contact support with the transaction ID, amount, time, and booking number.

### Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on booking status, cancellation timing, payment method, and operational costs already incurred. Approved refunds are usually processed to the original payment method, subject to payment provider and bank timelines.

## Tracking Your Shipment

### Live Tracking

Once a booking is active and location data is available, the tracking screen may show route progress, pickup/drop details, driver/truck details, ETA, distance remaining, and shipment status.

Location updates depend on driver device connectivity, GPS permissions, network availability, and backend sync. Temporary delays in live location updates do not always mean the shipment has stopped.

### Share Tracking

If share tracking is enabled, you may generate a public tracking link for a shipment. Anyone with this link can view limited tracking information without logging in. Share the link only with trusted recipients.

### Pickup OTP

Some trips require a pickup OTP before loading starts. Share the OTP only with the assigned driver at the pickup point. Do not share OTPs through public channels.

## Booking Statuses

Common statuses include:

- Pending: Booking has been created and is awaiting confirmation or assignment.
- Confirmed: A driver or broker has accepted or confirmed the booking.
- In Progress: Shipment is active.
- Delivered: Shipment has reached the destination.
- Cancelled: Booking was cancelled by the client, driver, broker, or platform.
- Refunded: Payment refund has been initiated or completed where applicable.

Status wording may vary slightly in the app depending on backend updates.

## Cancellations

You may request cancellation from the booking flow where available or by contacting support. Cancellation charges may apply if a driver has already been assigned, the truck has reached pickup, loading has started, or operational costs have been incurred.

If a driver or broker cancels, the platform may try to reassign the booking or guide you through the next step.

## Saved Addresses

Saved addresses help you book faster. Keep address labels, contact names, phone numbers, landmarks, and instructions updated to avoid pickup or delivery delays.

## Payment Methods

You can save supported payment methods where available. Remove any payment method you no longer use. For security, the platform may rely on payment providers to store or tokenize card/payment details.

## Notifications

Notifications may include booking updates, payment reminders, shipment progress, chat alerts, and account activity. If notifications are not arriving:

- Check app notification permissions.
- Confirm your device has internet access.
- Refresh the notifications screen.
- Make sure you are logged in with the correct account.

## Chat and Communication

Booking chat may be available for shipment coordination. Use chat for trip-related communication only. Avoid sharing sensitive personal, financial, or OTP information unless the app specifically requires it for the shipment.

## Safety and Responsibility

Clients are responsible for providing accurate shipment details and ensuring the goods are legally transportable. Restricted, illegal, hazardous, or undeclared goods may be refused, cancelled, reported, or handled under applicable law.

Before pickup, confirm:

- Package type and quantity
- Weight and dimensions
- Loading/unloading requirements
- Contact person availability
- Access restrictions at pickup/drop
- Any invoice, e-way bill, permit, or document required for transport

## Common Issues

### I cannot find my booking

Check that you are logged in with the same account used to create the booking. Refresh My Bookings. If the issue continues, contact support with your registered mobile/email and payment details if any payment was made.

### The driver location is not updating

This may happen because of weak GPS, device battery settings, low network, or temporary sync delay. Refresh the tracking screen. If the shipment is delayed or unreachable, contact support.

### I selected the wrong address

Contact support as early as possible. Address changes after confirmation may affect price, route, driver assignment, and delivery timeline.

### The fare looks different from the estimate

Fare may change due to updated distance, route changes, truck availability, halting, express handling, payment stage, taxes, or manual correction by operations. Contact support if you need a breakdown.

### The shipment is delayed

Delays can happen due to traffic, weather, loading/unloading time, route restrictions, vehicle issues, permit checks, or customer-side availability. The tracking screen and notifications will show updates where available.

## Escalations

Escalate to support if:

- The driver is unreachable.
- The truck has not arrived after confirmation.
- Payment was deducted but not reflected.
- Shipment status is wrong.
- Goods are damaged, missing, or delivered to the wrong location.
- You suspect fraud, misuse, or unauthorized account access.

Support may ask for invoices, photos, delivery proof, chat history, payment references, or identity verification before resolving certain issues.
