# Asset readiness — 2026-10-15

The current bundle contains 42 PNGs: four client scenes and three driver scenes,
each exported in six device presets. See STANDARDS.md for official requirements,
and validation-report.json for file checks and remaining gaps.

The user chose to reuse the supplied WhatsApp-compressed Android phone captures,
585–640 px wide. Upscaling does not restore source detail. iPhone, iPad and Android
tablet versions are design drafts. Resizing a phone capture cannot establish the
native tablet or iOS experience.

Tablet exports omit marketing text, branding and route graphics. Phone UI keeps its
proportions on a neutral canvas; these are placeholders until real tablet captures
replace them. Do not submit them as proof of tablet support. The project enables
iPhone and iPad in TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY, so native iPad captures are needed.

The driver set has three distinct screens. This exceeds Google's basic listing
minimum but is short of its four-image app promotion and large-screen guidance.
One more authentic driver screen is needed. No duplicate or fictional state was added.

Images preserve booking IDs, addresses, cancelled trips and earnings values; review
them before public use. The truck picker retains Android navigation icons over its
bottom action. A fresh capture should correct that. The personal profile image is
excluded because of its email and malformed totals.

No video is generated from these stills. Studio ratings/age are placeholders.
Original-resolution device-specific captures are needed to finish submission.
